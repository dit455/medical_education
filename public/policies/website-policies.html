Content Archival Policy

Announcements, circulars and notices are kept on the home page while they are current. After their validity ends (or 6 months after publication, whichever is earlier), they are moved to the Archive, where they remain available for at least 3 years.

Content Review Policy

Content on this website is reviewed by the Board at least once every 3 months, and immediately whenever there is a change in examination schedules, rules or contact details.

Security Policy

Unauthorised attempts to upload or change information on this website are strictly prohibited and may be punishable under the Information Technology Act, 2000. Department and institution access is protected by role-based login, OTP verification and captcha.

Search Testing

Website search is tested every month for relevance, speed and results in each supported language (English, Hindi and Tamil). Problems found are fixed in the next update.

Contingency Management

The website is backed up regularly. In case of any disruption, the website will be restored from the latest backup at the earliest.
