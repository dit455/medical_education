Help

Viewing documents

  • PDF files: need a PDF reader such as Adobe Acrobat Reader (opens in a new tab) (free).
  • Excel templates (XLSX): open with Microsoft Excel, LibreOffice Calc or any compatible spreadsheet program.

Accessibility help

  • Use Tab / Shift+Tab to move between links and buttons, and Enter to open them.
  • Use the accessibility widget on the home page to change text size or contrast.
  • Screen readers such as NVDA (free), JAWS and TalkBack can be used with this website.

Department and Institution login

  • Choose your login type (Super Admin, Department or Institution), then enter your user name, password and the captcha shown.
  • Forgot your password? Use the "Forgot password?" link on the login page, or contact the helpdesk.
