Accessibility Statement

The Board of Medical Education & Board of Examinations in Nursing is committed to ensuring that this website is accessible to all users, irrespective of device, technology or ability. It has been built to follow the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0), the Digital Brand Identity Manual (DBIM) and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Features

  • "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • Full keyboard navigation with a visible focus outline.
  • Text alternatives for images; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Colour contrast checked against WCAG 2.1 AA.
  • Accessibility controls (text size, contrast) through the UX4G accessibility widget.
  • Consistent navigation, headings in logical order and descriptive link text.

Known limitations

  • Some downloadable templates are in spreadsheet format; accessible PDF versions are being prepared.
  • Indian-language versions of the website are being enabled through Bhashini.

If you face any difficulty in accessing any part of this website, please write to helpdesk@mtpg-rihs.py.gov.in or call 0413 – 2238306. Please mention the page address and the problem you faced.
