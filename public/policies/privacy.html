Privacy Policy

This website does not automatically capture any specific personal information from you (such as name, phone number or e-mail address) that allows us to identify you individually.

If this website requests you to provide personal information (for example, through the Feedback form), you will be informed of the particular purpose for which the information is gathered, and adequate security measures will be taken to protect it.

Information we collect

  • Feedback form: name, e-mail address, category and message – used only to respond to your query.
  • Department and Institution login (authorised users only): user name and session details – used only to provide access to the Examination Marks System.
  • Server logs: IP address, browser type, date and time of visit and pages visited – used only for statistics and security, never to identify individual users except where required by law.

Cookies and storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The login session uses browser session storage, which is cleared when you log out or close the browser.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any personally identifiable information volunteered on this website with any third party (public or private), except where required by law.

Cookie consent

When you first visit, a banner at the bottom of the page lets you accept, reject or customise non-essential storage. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer. Your choice is remembered for 180 days.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to access, correct or erase the personal data you have given us, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Write to the helpdesk; we will respond within 30 days.

Data location

All data is stored on servers located in India.

Questions about this policy: helpdesk@mtpg-rihs.py.gov.in
